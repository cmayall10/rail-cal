Put every league and cup fixture in your phone's calendar. Kick-off times and ground addresses included, and it keeps itself up to date when games move.

Add to my calendar

Choose Subscribe when your phone asks.

If that button does nothing

  • iPhone: Settings, Apps, Calendar, Accounts, Add Account, Other, Add Subscribed Calendar, then paste the address below.
  • Android: open calendar.google.com on a computer, Other calendars, +, From URL, paste the address below. It reaches your phone within a day.

Getting a reminder before kick-off

  • iPhone: reminders come through on their own.
  • Google: hover the calendar in the left-hand list, three dots, Settings, Event notifications, add one for 3 hours before.

Worth knowing

  • Rearranged fixtures reach you within a day, so anything called off on the morning of a game will come through WhatsApp first.
  • Don't save the file to your phone. That gives you a snapshot that never changes.
